Job Summary
Assist in all aspects of native plant greenhouse and nursery production operations, including: site and material preparation, plant production and maintenance, and general operational maintenance and upkeep.
Reports To Alisha Bretzman, Owner of Piney Island Native Plants

  • Good Conduct and Academic Standing with the college

Working Conditions/Physical Requirements
The majority of work is anticipated to take place in outdoor/exposed work environments, including climate-controlled greenhouse and outdoor environments. Some work may occur in an indoor office environment. Employees needs to be comfortable/able to:
  • Work in variety of exposed environmental conditions, including but not limited to, heat, sun, cold, precipitation, ice, mud, wind, and dust.
  • Bend, reach, and lift up to 60lbs at short intervals
  • Complete physically arduous, repetitive tasks for extended intervals
  • Work with and around chemicals, fertilizers, and other potentially hazardous substances used in commercial growing operations.
  • Employee WILL NOT be using or applying any Restricted-Use Pesticides.

Responsibility/Duty
Employee will assist in all aspects of commercial native plant greenhouse and nursery operations. Specific duties and tasks will vary, and change, depending on the season, species under production, and other operational needs.

Responsibilities and duties (may) include:
  • Site and material preparation. Tasks may include:
    • Repair/replace fencing
    • Install mulch and/or ground fabric
    • Clean/prepare greenhouse and grow-out area
    • Wash/sterilize production containers
    • Stock production containers with growing media

  • Native plant propagation and production. Tasks may include:
    • Seed native plant seeds in necessary containers according to specific propagation protocol
    • Maintain seedbeds for germination
    • Maintain (watering, fertilizing, monitoring) plants in accordance with specific protocols and procedures
    • Keep detailed records according to established inventory systems

  • General operational maintenance and upkeep:
    • Maintain sanitary and hygienic production areas; clean production materials and tools
    • Monitor, treat, and document pests in accordance with Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Plan protocols and practices
    • Assist in researching native plant physiology/ecology and existing propagation methods
    • Assist the development and implementation of propagation experiments, protocols, or methods
