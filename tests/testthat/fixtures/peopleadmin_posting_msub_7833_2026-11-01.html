Duties and Responsibilities
DUTIES and RESPONSIBILITIES:
Duties include, but may not be limited to:
  • Scrubbing, mopping, stripping, waxing, disinfecting, and polishing hard surface floors
  • Vacuuming, spotting, and extracting carpeted floors
  • Dusting and polishing furniture and horizontal surfaces
  • Washing interior windows, entrance glass, and mirrors Clean, disinfect, and polishing restroom and shower fixtures
  • Restock restroom supplies
  • Cleaning blackboards, whiteboards, smart boards, and erasers
  • Empty and clean trash receptacles
  • Replacing light bulbs and fluorescent tubes
  • Moving chairs, tables, and floor machinery
  • Handling commercial (industrial) cleaning chemicals
  • Moving supplies up and down stairs
  • Cleaning Kitchenettes
  • Cleaning Student Family Housing units
  • Cleaning Residence Halls


PHYSICAL DEMANDS: The position involves physical demands associated with lifting objects up to 50 lbs with or without help, bending, climbing stairs, operating commercial cleaning equipment, and climbing ladders. This position involves hazards associated with exposure to hazardous materials (cleaning chemicals, blood borne pathogens), unpleasant sights and odors and involves exposure to unfavorable weather conditions.
