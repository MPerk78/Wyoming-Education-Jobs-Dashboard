Wanna Volunteer?

Dear GS Families, 

We are so grateful for the many families in our district who are eager to volunteer and support our students. Your willingness to give your time and energy is invaluable to our school community.

If you plan to volunteer consistently in one of our athletic programs or a classroom on a frequent basis throughout the school year, we require a few additional steps to ensure the safety and well-being of our students. Please complete our Volunteer Information Form, as well as a completed background check. Please note, volunteers are responsible for initiating background checks.

You can return your completed form and your completed background check can be sent to our District Office. If you have any further questions, please feel free to contact Rhonda Martin.

Thank you again for your commitment to our students!
