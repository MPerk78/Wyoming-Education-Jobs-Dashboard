WCSD#1 Student Teaching Opportunities
WCSD#1 welcomes any future teachers that are looking for a future in teaching to apply for our Student Teaching Program. Enjoy the wonderful Black Hills while following your passion!
 
If you are interested in our Student Teaching Program, please contact the Building Principal that you are interested in working with. Their contact information is below.
  1. Brandy Holmes Elementary Principal
