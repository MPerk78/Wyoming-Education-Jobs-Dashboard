Subs Needed

WESTON COUNTY SCHOOL DISTRICT #7

WCSD #7 is currently accepting applications for:

Substitute employees in all areas – bus driving (will provide paid training); custodial; cook; special education aides; and classroom teacher subs.

         Substitute teachers – must hold a valid substitute permit from the Wyoming Professional Teachers Standards Board or a valid Wyoming Teaching Certificate (background check required during the certification process).  Requires the completion of a district employment application.   

o   $140 per day for those holding only a PTSB Wyoming Substitute Certificate

o   $145 per day for those holding an active PTSB Wyoming Teaching Certificate

o   $150 per day for Weston 7 retired teachers with an active PTSB Teaching Certificate

        Substitute paraprofessional, food service & custodial staff – requires a completed application and fingerprinting for a background check.  Hourly rate varies by department. 

        Substitute bus drivers – requires the completion of a district employment application, fingerprinting for a background check, a DOT physical, passing a pre-employment drug test, and a CDL license.  The district will provide paid hourly training to those needing to obtain a CDL permit or needing to add the bus driver endorsement to their current CDL.  Pay rate $20/hour. 

To apply please stop by the business office at 804 Willow to pick up an application.  If you have any questions, please call Brandy at 307-468-2461.

JOB OPENING

WESTON COUNTY SCHOOL DISTRICT #7

Weston County School District #7 is currently accepting applications for the following position for the

2026-2027 school year:

Paraprofessional - Technology Aide

WCSD7 is seeking a full-time Paraprofessional to support our High School students and provide technology support throughout our District.

Primary Responsibilities

• Must have a current substitute certificate or ability to be obtained in timely manner.

• Supervise and help students utilizing the High School library and other assigned areas.

• Provide tutoring and academic support to students as needed.

• Assist students and staff with basic technology needs and troubleshooting.

• Assist with library organization, resources, and day-to-day operations.

Compensation & Benefits

This is a full-time, benefit-eligible position. Benefits include:

• District health-insurance benefits

• Retirement through Wyoming Retirement System

• Paid leave

If interested in applying please submit a district application, cover letter, and two letters of

recommendation. Applications may be located online at www.weston7.org or picked up at the WCSD7

Administration Building, located at:

804 Willow Street

Upton, WY 82730

For questions, please contact Gina Barritt at 307-468-2461
