Sheridan County School District #3 is a place where educators come to do deeply meaningful work in a setting that feels personal, supportive, and refreshingly human. With small class sizes and a four-day school week, teachers have space to build genuine relationships with students, collaborate thoughtfully with colleagues, and design instruction that reflects both professional expertise and individual learners' needs. SCSD#3 is also simply a great place to work: staff are valued, voices are heard, and the work environment balances high expectations with genuine care. For educators who want to make a real difference and be part of a tight-knit rural school community with big aspirations, Clearmont is a place where your impact will be felt every single day. It's a GREAT Day to be a PANTHER!!!
